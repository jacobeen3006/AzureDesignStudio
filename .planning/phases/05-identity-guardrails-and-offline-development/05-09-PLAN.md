<objective>
Only a Deploy.Approver can approve a plan, self-approval is refused as its own rule unless the tenant allows it, the requester can deploy someone else's approval, and decommission approvals need the role.

Purpose: Success Criterion 4, approval half (IDA-06).

Decisions implemented:
- D-02 (costly, settled): approval requires Deploy.Approver; self-approval is a separate rule governed by the audited per-tenant setting (default off).
- D-04 (costly, settled): a personal-account scope implies Deploy.Approver; the single-user Desktop host implies Guardrail.Admin, Deploy.Approver and self-approval; organizational tenants need real roles and the setting.
- D-06 (costly, settled): decommission approvals need tenant scope and Deploy.Approver only.
- D-07 (costly, settled): the requester is recorded on the gate entry and the approval row; the requester may deploy an approval made by someone else; approver equal to requester is refused unless the tenant setting allows it.

Output: the approval rules in DeployService, the matrix tests, and an in-process proof for the Desktop host.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-05-27 | Elevation of privilege | Approving a plan without Deploy.Approver | high | mitigate | caller.CanApprove checked first in ApprovePlan; role matrix tests over service and host |
| T-05-28 | Elevation of privilege | The requester approving their own change, defeating separation of duties | high | mitigate | Separate rule PLAN_SELF_APPROVAL_REFUSED unless the audited tenant setting or the single-user host; the matrix tests cover every combination; the setting is changed only by an administrator and audited (plan 05-07) |
